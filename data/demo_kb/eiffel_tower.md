# Eiffel Tower reference

The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It was constructed from 1887 to 1889 as the entrance to the 1889 World's Fair. The structure is named after engineer Gustave Eiffel, whose company designed and built it. The tower is 330 metres tall after the installation of its current antenna.

The tower was not built in 1920 and it is not located in Rome. It has become a global cultural icon of France and one of the most recognizable structures in the world.
